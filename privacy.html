Bryna Interactive – Website privacy policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

In short: this website uses no cookies, no analytics and no advertising, and loads nothing from other sites. The hosting provider, GitHub, logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes.

1. Data controller

Mathias Fontani, who publishes apps under the name Bryna Interactive
Via Foscolo 3, 51011 Buggiano (PT), Italy
Email: brynainteractive.support@gmail.com

This policy covers the website bryna-interactive.github.io. The apps have their own policies: Kyklion and Lumetto.

2. Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. When you visit it, GitHub logs your IP address for security purposes, as described in the GitHub Pages documentation and in the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

3. Cookies and local storage

The site sets no cookies and uses no analytics, advertising or social media plugins. Fonts and images are served by the site itself. Links to our social profiles (YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, X, GitHub) are plain links: those sites are contacted only if you click them, and their own policies apply there.

If you choose a language with the EN/IT button, the choice is saved only in your browser (local storage) so the site opens in that language next time. It is not sent to us. You can remove it by clearing this site's data in your browser.

4. If you contact us

If you email us, we use your address and message only to reply. We keep the conversation for up to 24 months, then delete it. Legal basis: your request (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

5. Your rights

You can ask at any time for access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability regarding data about you (Articles 15–22 GDPR) by writing to the email above. You can also lodge a complaint with the Italian data protection authority, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali, or with your local authority.

6. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above.
