Lumetto – Sample comics

Lumetto does not include any comics. To try it, you can download these free episodes of Pepper&Carrot as CBZ files and open them in the app.

  • Episode 34: The Knighting of Shichimi (CBZ, 8.0 MB)
  • Episode 35: The Reflection (CBZ, 6.9 MB)
  • Episode 36: The Surprise Attack (CBZ, 9.5 MB)

How to use them: download the files to your device (for example the Download folder), open Lumetto and add that folder to the library.
